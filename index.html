Work

I work for a small water and wastewater utility, Consolidated Utilities. I used to type thousands and thousands of numbers into Excel by hand back when I worked at the plant, so I proposed software to digitize the whole workflow to my Uncle Kim, an Operator.

My motivation was simply to make people's jobs easier, faster and more pleasant. Six years in, I'm realizing how vital a real database is - it's opening up possibilities I didn't see at the start. I love working at a small company where I can watch my work make a direct difference.
